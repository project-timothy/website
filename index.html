Mission

Those who carry the gospel are sent to preach the good news, to give themselves to prayer, and to steward the money well for the kingdom. Timothy exists to make that easier: modern tools that carry the practical work faithfully, so the missionary can do what they were sent to do.

Vision

Every missionary on earth, whether sent by a large organization, a small church, or going out on their own, has a faithful back office: one that costs nothing to run, keeps the books in order, tells them each morning the one thing that needs them, and never stands between a giver and the work. Administration is no longer the reason a called worker comes home.

Values

What a change has to pass

Six tests, each tied to a verse, that any feature or decision is measured against before it ships.

MATTHEW 6:33

Kingdom first

Does it help the gospel go out and the saints be built up? A feature that only makes the software more impressive fails.

LUKE 16:10

Faithful in little

Every gift accounted for to the cent. Timothy never holds funds, never takes a cut, and never moves money on its own.

EPHESIANS 4:12

Equipping the saints

A pastor, a spouse, or a volunteer can run it well, with no specialist and no subscription.

ACTS 6:2-4

Serving, not ruling

Tim carries the tables so the workers can carry the word. A missionary's family finances stay with the missionary.

PROVERBS 27:17

Iron sharpens iron

Open source and cooperative: missionaries, churches, sending organizations, and developers at one table.

2 CORINTHIANS 8:21

Honorable in the sight of all

Plain reports, sober words, numbers that tie, nothing that cannot be shown to a giver.

How Tim works

You talk to Tim. He does the work behind it.

A unit is one missionary or missionary family, the smallest thing with its own account, budget, and reports. Timothy runs one instance per unit.

You talk to Tim or type to him, the way a person talks to a capable assistant, and he answers with the facts and does the work behind them: filing the receipt, building the report, tracking support against budget, drafting the thank-you, watching the calendar of visas and filings, and preparing every request so a person can say yes in seconds. It is built as modules a unit switches on as needed, on a back-office engine that already runs unattended in production: document intake, approval cards, a nightly audit, and a sealed monthly close.

Open and shared

One tractor, a standard hitch, and every farm's own field

Timothy lives in public repositories at github.com/project-timothy, and it will stay public, so a missionary anywhere can run the whole thing on a five-dollar server, and any technically minded volunteer can help.

How people can help

Iron sharpens iron. The best ideas win, whoever brings them.

Missionaries

The listening tour is step one. An hour describing a real month is the most valuable contribution there is.

Churches & volunteers

Hosting. A church with one technically minded member can run Timothy for the missionaries it supports.

Developers

A module, an adapter to a system we haven't met yet, a translation, tests against the synthetic unit.

Sending organizations

The systems table, a sample statement with the numbers changed, and thirty minutes with the person who answers finance questions.
